This lets VoxAgent Studio upload new episodes to the channel as private videos. Nothing is published publicly by it; your password is never shared, and you can remove the access any time in your Google Account (Security → Third-party access).

  2. If Google asks which channel or account, choose VoxAgent.
  3. If you see “Google hasn’t verified this app”: Advanced → Go to VoxAgent Studio, then Continue / Allow.
  4. You come back here with a code. Copy it and send it to Olena (it works only once, for 10 minutes).
