VoxAgent 2.0

Two minds, one question about AI.

VoxAgent is a calm debate podcast about how artificial intelligence actually works. In every episode two AI hosts, Arjun, an optimistic machine-learning engineer, and Beatrice, a dry British skeptic, take one real engineering question and argue it out, with everyday analogies and checked sources.

VoxAgent Studio is the private production tool behind the show. It researches a topic, writes and fact-checks the script, voices it, renders the video and uploads it to the channel's own YouTube account as a private video for review.
